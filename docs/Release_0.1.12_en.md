# Verb Room 0.1.12

- **Positioned dry:** adjust the balance between spatial direct sound and the full room while preserving source position, distance and listener cues. Available in Mix; enabled for new instances. Existing projects retain their original Dry/Wet behaviour.
- **Lock pan:** prevent accidental map panning while selecting and editing surfaces.
- **Scene outliner:** select, rename, hide and lock individual surfaces or groups. Hidden surfaces remain acoustically active.
- **Linked constructions:** group walls and zones, move them together, or unlink them without changing the geometry. Grouping and metadata edits support Undo.
- **Saved organization:** surface names, groups and visibility/locking are stored in scenes, projects and complete presets. New complete presets use format 2 and require version 0.1.12 or later with format 2 support; older presets and project states still load.

Windows x64 build. Close your DAW before replacing the entire `Verb Room.vst3` bundle.

See [the feature guide](Spatial_editor_0.1.12_en.md) for controls and compatibility details.
